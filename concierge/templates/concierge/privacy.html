This page explains what happens to your information when you contact {{ host_name }} over WhatsApp about services during your stay. It is a small operation run by one host, and the short version is: your details are used to arrange what you asked for, and nothing else.

What is collected

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • Your WhatsApp profile name, if your privacy settings share it
  • The content of the messages you send, and the replies sent to you
  • The date and time of each message

Nothing is collected from you automatically by visiting this website. There are no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no cookies used for tracking.

How it is used

Your messages are used to understand what you are asking for and to arrange it with a local service provider — an excursion operator, a driver, a restaurant, and so on. A record of each request is kept so the booking can be followed up and any problem resolved.

Messages may be processed by an automated system to categorise the request (for example, recognising that a message is about an island excursion rather than a taxi). No decision affecting you is made solely by that system — a human host is involved in every booking.

Who it is shared with

  • The service provider handling your request. They receive what you asked for and the details needed to fulfil it. Your phone number is deliberately not passed on — messages are relayed through this service, so the provider replies to {{ host_name }}, not to you directly.
  • WhatsApp (Meta). Messages travel over WhatsApp, so Meta processes them as the messaging provider, under their own privacy policy.

Your information is never sold, rented, or used for advertising, and it is not shared with anyone else except where the law requires it.

How long it is kept

Booking records and their message history are kept while they may still be needed — to handle a follow-up question, a refund, or a dispute — and are deleted when they no longer serve that purpose. You can ask for your records to be deleted sooner at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. You can also simply stop messaging — nothing is sent to you unless you start a conversation or have an active booking.
